Citizen science

Every trip is a chance to document the road

OpenRoad isn't only for inspectors. Drivers, cyclists, pedestrians, volunteers, and advocates can all record what they encounter and turn it into structured data.

When many people travel the same corridors, their observations add up. Repeated observations from multiple users create stronger crowd signals — helping agencies identify locations that may need further review or improvement, backed by evidence from the field rather than a single report.

How crowd signal works

One observation is a data point. Many observations of the same location, from different people at different times, become a signal. OpenRoad keeps each observation structured and geolocated, so agencies can weigh corroborating reports and prioritize where to look next.
